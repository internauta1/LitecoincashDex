# LitecoinCash DEX Next — Third-party Identity Checks

Version: 3 · 9 November 2026

A local wallet login does not create a custodial account with LitecoinCash Brasil. This community distribution does not operate a corporate identity-verification programme.

Optional external services, such as fiat payment providers, may require identity checks under their own rules. Review the named provider, its domain, privacy notice and terms before submitting personal documents. The community cannot promise eligibility or the outcome of those checks.

Never submit a seed phrase, private key, wallet password or RPC password as an identity document. For community questions, use the [published channels](https://litecoincash.com.br/comunidade), without sending wallet secrets.
